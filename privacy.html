N3XI0M Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information N3XI0M may process, why it may be processed, how it may be protected, and the role of third-party services.

Effective: 15 September 2026 Version: 1.0

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the service, N3XI0M may process technical information such as request timestamps, IP-related security information, browser or device details, error logs, service status information, and abuse-prevention data.

Community platforms may separately process account information under their own privacy policies.

2. Why Information May Be Processed

  • operating and maintaining services;
  • detecting abuse, attacks, fraud, and security incidents;
  • diagnosing faults and reliability problems;
  • providing support;
  • enforcing community and service rules;
  • meeting applicable legal obligations.

3. Security Logging

N3XI0M may use traffic filtering, rate limiting, access logs, error logs, and automated security controls where reasonably necessary to protect infrastructure and users.

4. Third-Party Services

N3XI0M may rely on third-party services such as hosting providers, Discord, GitHub, content delivery services, or similar infrastructure. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Minimisation and Retention

N3XI0M aims to retain only information reasonably needed for operational, security, support, legal, or abuse-prevention purposes, and not longer than necessary for those purposes.

6. Sensitive Information

Users should never send passwords, authentication tokens, cookies, private keys, payment secrets, or other credentials through ordinary support channels.

7. Children and Age Requirements

N3XI0M is intended for users aged 13 or older. Features may be restricted where age, law, or platform rules require it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other forms of control over personal information.

9. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as N3XI0M services change. Material revisions should be reflected by a new effective date or version.

10. Contact

Privacy-related questions can be raised through the official N3XI0M support channels.
