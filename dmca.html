Enforcement may include DMCA takedown notices, hosting-provider reports, repository reports, platform abuse reports, removal requests, cease-use requests, or other remedies available under applicable law.

A copyright complaint should identify the protected work, identify the allegedly infringing material, provide enough information to locate it, include contact information, and explain the basis for the complaint.

Copyright tools should only be used in good faith. False or knowingly misleading copyright claims may carry legal consequences.

7. Counter-Notices

Where applicable law or a platform process permits a counter-notice, affected users may use the relevant provider's counter-notification procedure.

Open-source software remains subject to its applicable license. This policy does not override rights granted by an open-source license.

9. Contact

Copyright concerns should be raised through official N3XI0M support channels with enough evidence for the issue to be reviewed.
